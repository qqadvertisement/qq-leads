# ICP — who counts as a lead

QQ Studio builds restaurant websites for independent Chicago restaurants. The **website** is
the wedge (the gap is visible, so it's the opener). The **playable game + loyalty engine** —
which collects customer emails and drives repeat visits — is the differentiator that earns a
monthly retainer, and it is revealed after the reply, never led with.

Outreach is online-only, primarily Instagram DM.

## Include

Independent, owner-operated restaurants (1–3 locations) in the Chicago metro that have a
**visible online-presence gap** and are **reachable** — i.e. active on Instagram.

Qualifying gaps, one of exactly these five (categorical — the dashboard charts them):

| Gap type | Means |
|---|---|
| `No website` | Google listing links nowhere, or only to a Facebook page |
| `Broken links` | Broken site, dead IG-bio link, dead domain, dead order buttons |
| `No menu online` | Menu not findable anywhere online |
| `Weak / outdated site` | Site exists but clearly neglected or template-default |
| `Not mobile-friendly` | No viewport handling, desktop-only layout |

## Exclude

- Chains and franchises — corporate handles marketing, no local decision-maker
- Restaurant-group-owned spots, for the same reason
- Anywhere with a solid, modern, mobile-friendly site (no visible gap to open with)
- Permanently or temporarily closed spots
- Delivery-only / ghost kitchens with no dine-in identity
- Anything not findable on Google **and** Instagram — can't reach, can't personalize

## Scoring (1–5)

| | |
|---|---|
| **+2** | Clear, screenshot-able website gap — the thing you lead with |
| **+1** | Active on Instagram (~last 30 days) — reachable, and the owner cares about presence |
| **+1** | Independent / owner-operated (1–3 locations) — decision-maker directly reachable |
| **+1** | Can plausibly afford a small monthly retainer: busy (lots of reviews / visible foot traffic) **or** recently opened or renovated (already investing — a "why now") |
| **−1** | Already has a modern, mobile-friendly site |
| **−1** | Can't confirm currently open, or can't find on Google/IG at all |

## Edge cases already ruled on

- **Snack/drink stands** (aguas frescas, popcorn counters, ice cream) — borderline. Flag for
  a human ICP-fit call rather than scoring them as restaurants.
- **Deli / catering / carryout with no dine-in seating** — out. Confirm dine-in identity
  before adding.
- **A business with 2+ sibling LLCs at different addresses** — treat as a small group, out.
  City business-license data is a good way to spot this.
- **A restaurant that opened in the last ~60 days with a placeholder site** — assume mid-build
  and check before pitching. See `gap-verification.md`.
- **A page on somebody else's site is not a website.** A subpage on a parent nonprofit, a
  landlord's or cultural centre's site, a builder subdomain (`*.godaddysites.com`,
  `*.business.site`), an auto-generated delivery-platform page, or a Facebook page in the
  website field — all of these are `No website`, not `Weak / outdated site`. Treating one as
  a site the owner controls understates the gap, and the pitch lands weaker for it: "your menu
  page is missing" is a much smaller idea than "you don't have a site of your own."
